The Intersection of Hardware and Code

I am a software engineer with a distinct passion for physical systems. While many developers focus solely on browser-based software, I have dedicated substantial time to understanding how software interacts with actual microcontrollers, sensors, analog-to-digital converters, and industrial touchscreens.

At Curves and Contours LLP, I engineer full-screen graphical applications in PyQt6 that run on Raspberry Pi Compute Modules inside physical medical therapy units. These machines require high-contrast clinical themes, touch keypad validation, safety cutoff monitoring, and continuous systemd service automation so the device operates as a trustworthy dedicated appliance.

Previously, at uCertify, I participated in structured technical content ingestion pipelines, verifying digital educational courseware against strict validation criteria. During an internship at Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) in Kanpur, I analyzed aircraft instrumentation, avionics communication architectures, and explored applied computer vision.

Engineering Philosophy

1. Resilience over Hype: I prioritize predictable error boundaries, defensive parameter validation, and robust system recovery over fragile bleeding-edge frameworks.

2. The Appliance Experience: When software runs on a dedicated kiosk or embedded display, it must feel like an appliance—no visible operating system crashes, no accidental minimize shortcuts, and clean boot-up directly into the workflow.

3. Transparent Attribution: I communicate exactly what I built, the challenges faced, and the real hardware constraints involved, avoiding inflated or fabricated claims.
